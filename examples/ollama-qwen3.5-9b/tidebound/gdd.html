Tidebound (example GDD)

Pitch

A single-player roguelike deckbuilder set on a drowned coastline. Each run you sail between islands, build a deck of tide-themed cards, and fight sea creatures on a board that floods and drains every few turns, changing which cards you can play.

Core loop

  • The core loop must revolve around the tide mechanic where the board floods and drains every few turns, changing which cards are playable [1].
  • Proposal: Players will sail between islands to craft a unique deck of tide-themed cards that adapt to the shifting water levels.
  • Open question: How can we balance the flood/drain cycle so it doesn't disrupt the flow state described in similar games like Balatro? [2].
  • Proposal: We will include over 220 cards across multiple starter decks to ensure no playthrough is ever the same as Monster Train does. [3].
  • Open question: Should we implement daily climbs or mysterious events like Slay the Spire to encourage replayability? [4].

Target player

  • The designer wants the tide mechanic to be the one thing reviewers remember: the board floods and drains every few turns, changing which cards are playable. [5]
  • Proposal: We will focus on a single-player experience where players craft a unique deck to encounter bizarre creatures and discover relics of immense power.
  • Open question: How can we balance the dynamic tide mechanic with the need for deep stat tracking without overwhelming new players?
  • Proposal: The game should offer adjustable text sizes and full controller support to match the accessibility standards set by successful single-player deckbuilders. [6]
  • Open question: What is the optimal number of starter decks needed to provide variety without diluting the core tide-themed gameplay loop?
  • Proposal: We will include an in-depth stats screen allowing players to study their best hands and most used cards after every single run.

Reference games

  • Slay the Spire fused card games and roguelikes together to make the best single player deckbuilder we could. [7]
  • Balatro is a hypnotically satisfying deckbuilder where you play illegal poker hands, discover game-changing jokers, and trigger adrenaline-pumping, outrageous combos. [8]
  • Monster Train features over 220 cards, build up your deck from 10 different starterdecks 5 monster clans with each very different gameplay. [9]
  • Proposal: The new game will replace the static board of Slay the Spire with a flooded coastline where tiles become unusable when submerged.
  • Open question: How can we implement a tide mechanic that changes card availability without making the player feel punished for failing to adapt quickly enough?

Monetization

  • Slay the Spire was priced at $24.99 on the US store when it released on Jan 23, 2019. [10]
  • Proposal: We will price Tidebound at $19.99 to match Inscryption's accessible indie strategy tier while maintaining high perceived value.
  • Open question: How many DLC expansions should we plan for given that Monster Train lists 2 DLCs and Balatro lists only 1?
  • The designer wants the tide mechanic to be the one thing reviewers remember because it changes which cards are playable every few turns. [11]
  • Proposal: We will include Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud support to align with standard features found in Slay the Spire and Monster Train.
