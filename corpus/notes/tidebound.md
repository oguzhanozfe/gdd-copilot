# Tidebound: team notes (example user-supplied notes)

These notes stand in for what a real team would paste in. They describe the team's own constraints, not the market.

- The team is three people: one programmer, one artist, one designer.
- Target release window is 18 months from the start of production.
- First platform is PC on Steam; Steam Deck verification is a goal, mobile is out of scope for launch.
- The team wants runs to last 30 to 45 minutes so a full run fits a lunch break.
- The team has no budget for live operations or servers after launch.
- The designer wants the tide mechanic to be the one thing reviewers remember: the board floods and drains every few turns, changing which cards are playable.
