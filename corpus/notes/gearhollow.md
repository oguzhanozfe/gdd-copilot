# Gearhollow: team notes (example user-supplied notes)

These notes stand in for what a real team would paste in. They describe the team's own constraints, not the market.

- The team is five people and has shipped one small puzzle game before.
- The game must be playable with a gamepad on the couch as well as mouse and keyboard.
- The team wants no combat and no fail state; players should never lose a factory.
- Target session length is 20 to 60 minutes, with progress saved at any time.
- The team plans a premium price and is undecided about paid expansions.
- Seasonal festivals in the village are planned as free content updates.
